# **Glossary**

## Abbreviations

| **AC** | **Armor Class**, indicates the level of passive protection a creature receives from its skin or armor. |
| **LV** | **Level**, can mean the level of a skill, an [evocation](/en/runes/evocation), or a character. |
| **MP** | **Mana Points**, the unit of measure for arcane energy. |
| **HP** | **Health Points**, indicate how much damage a creature can suffer before receiving a [wound](/en/attributes/health). |

## Terminology

| **Action** | The resource that regulates how many actions a character can perform in a turn during combat. Each creature starts its turn with 3 actions. |
| **Reaction** | An action used outside one's turn to act in response to another creature's action in combat. |
| **Creature** | A living being capable of actively interacting with the surrounding environment. |
| **Evoker** | A person or creature able to control their mana sufficiently to produce visible effects in the external world. |
| **Mana** | An ethereal and mutable substance with immense potential but difficult to realize. It is controllable by certain creatures and can be shaped by runes to manifest one of its innumerable forms on command. |
