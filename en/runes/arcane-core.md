# **Arcane Core**

The arcane core is a magic object created by concentrating a chosen amount of mana into a single point and is necessary for any evocation process. Generated by concentrating the evoker's **mana**, the arcane core is characterized by significant instability and, in the absence of the evoker's control, dissipates instantly into the air.

## **Arcane Core Characteristics**

- **Instability**: Without the support of specific techniques, an arcane core deteriorates rapidly. To stabilize it, expert evokers resort to **orichalcum circuits**, magic materials that allow the core to persist.
- **Immutability**: Once created, the arcane core cannot be further empowered.
- **Mana Quantity**: The amount of mana an evoker can insert into an arcane core is limited by their [**arcane proficiency**](/en/attributes/arcane-proficiency), which represents their affinity and mastery of the arcane arts.

## **Arcane Core Creation**

Most evokers generate arcane cores with the same characteristics, concentrating mana in a uniform and raw way, resulting in the following evocation:

* Shape: **Contact**<br>    Applies its effects **exclusively to creatures in contact with the point of [evocation](./evocation)**, which are considered primary targets.

* Substance: **Pure Mana**<br>    Mana in its purest form. If it comes into contact with a creature or a vessel capable of storing mana, all present mana can be **absorbed**, up to maximum capacity. Otherwise, it dissipates into the air without effect.

In special cases, an evoker extremely skilled in **controlling their mana** can generate a core with specific properties and characteristics at their discretion, mimicking the capabilities that would normally be conferred only through runes. These advanced evokers are known as **innate evokers**: they are able to evoke complex and customized effects without the need for runes, fully mastering the nature and behavior of their mana. This rare ability distinguishes the innate evoker as an individual capable of adapting their core depending on situations and needs, making it possible to create sophisticated evocations more quickly and without the need for orichalcum or runes.

## **Evocation Process**

1. **Core Creation**: To create an arcane core, the evoker concentrates a part of their mana, thus establishing a temporary energy reserve ready for use.
2. **Stabilization in the Circuit**: Using an orichalcum circuit, the evoker stabilizes the core, allowing mana to flow and interact with the connected runes. The mana's properties will be modified by the **runes** traversed, based on their geometry.
3. **Rune Activation**: The core traverses the runes, assuming the magic properties defined by their design. Each rune influences the evocation based on its [**grade**](./quality), ranging from Grade 0 (damaged) to Grade 5 (perfect), and based on the core mana, which determines the level of the evoked effect.
4. **Evocation**: Once the runes have been traversed, mana is released into the air as a tangible magic effect. This effect, now stabilized and empowered by the runes, manifests in the environment according to the imparted properties, bringing the desired evocation to life.
