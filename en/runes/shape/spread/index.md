# **Spread**

Hits in an arc from the point of origin, inflicting damage to creatures within the area of effect.

| **Primary Targets**   | Creatures at the center of the spread          |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures in the area                        |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 300 mana                                  |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                   |

## Effect
The rune hits in an arc extending from the point of origin. The width of the arc and the length of the effect depend on the rune's grade. Creatures at the center of the arc are considered primary targets, while all other creatures within the area are secondary targets.
