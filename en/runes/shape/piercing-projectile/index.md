# **Piercing Projectile**

Launches a projectile that travels in a straight line, passing through all targets and minor obstacles it encounters.

| **Primary Targets**   | First object hit                     |
| **Secondary Targets** | All targets in the trajectory        |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 200 mana                                  |
| **Defense Mod.**        | DEX                                       |

## Effect
The projectile travels in a straight line, passing through all targets and minor obstacles along its trajectory. It dissipates only if it hits a significant obstacle or at the end of its maximum range.
