# **Shape Runes**

Shape runes define the dimensions and capacity of the pulse. Examples of shape runes are:

- [Projectile](./projectile): Transforms mana into a throwable projectile
- [Explosion](./small-explosion/): Destabilizes the pulse, dispersing energy in all directions
- [Hollow Projectile](./hollow-projectile/): A projectile containing a secondary pulse inside
