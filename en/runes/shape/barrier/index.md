# **Barrier**

Generates a large barrier covering a wide area around the point of origin.

| **Primary Targets**   | Creatures or objects intersecting the barrier at the moment of appearance |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures or objects crossing it while active                 |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 700 mana                                                                |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                                                 |

## Effect
The evocation manifests as a **spherical dome with a radius of 3 meters** around the point of origin, which persists for a number of turns relative to the rune's grade. Targets **inside or outside the barrier suffer no effects** until they cross it.

## Quality

| Grade 0 | Grade 1 | Grade 2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Duration 1 turn | Duration 2 turns | Duration 3 turns | Duration 4 turns | Duration 5 turns | Duration 6 turns |
