# **Large Explosion**

Generates a large-scale area-of-effect explosion, hitting all creatures present in the range of action.

| **Primary Targets**   | Center of the sphere and within half the radius       |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures in the area and not covered                 |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 800 mana                                         |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                          |

## Effect
Explodes from the point of origin, expanding uniformly and hitting all creatures in the area. The explosion ignores minor covers but is blocked by significant solid obstacles.
