# **Wide Ray**

Launches a broad ray that hits all creatures along its trajectory and those near the path.

| **Primary Targets**   | Creatures in the ray's trajectory     |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures within 1m of the trajectory       |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 800 mana                                  |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                   |

## Effect
The rune generates a ray that propagates in a straight line, hitting all creatures found directly along its path. The ray continues until it encounters a significant solid obstacle. Creatures within 1 meter of the ray's trajectory are considered secondary targets and suffer the ray's effect.
