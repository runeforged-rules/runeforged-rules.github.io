# **Homing Projectile**

Marks a target and then launches a projectile that chases it until hit, unless it exceeds maximum range.

| **Primary Targets**   | Marked target                           |
| **Secondary Targets** | None                                       |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 300 mana                                      |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                       |

## Effect
Marks a specific target. The launched projectile chases it until hit, dodging obstacles in the path. If it exceeds range without hitting, it dissipates into the air without producing further effects.

## Quality

| Grade 0   | Grade 1   | Grade 2   | Grade 3   | Grade 4   | Grade 5   |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Range 10m | Range 20m | Range 30m | Range 40m | Range 50m | Range 60m |
