# **Wall**

Generates a wall extending from the sides of the point of origin and persisting for a certain time.

| **Primary Targets**   | Creatures or objects present in the area at the moment of appearance |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures or objects attempting to cross it while active |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 500 mana                                               |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                                |

## Effect
The evocation creates a **1 meter** wide wall centered at the point of origin, which persists for a number of turns relative to the rune's grade. The wall remains anchored to the point of origin and follows its movements.

## Quality

| Grade 0 | Grade 1 | Grade 2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Duration 1 turn | Duration 2 turns | Duration 3 turns | Duration 4 turns | Duration 5 turns | Duration 6 turns |
