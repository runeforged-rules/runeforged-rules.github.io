# **Explosion**

Generates an area-of-effect explosion, inflicting damage to all creatures present within the explosion's radius.

| **Primary Targets**   | Center of the sphere                        |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures in an area and not covered         |
| **Base Cost per LV**         | 500 mana                                  |
| **Defense Mod.**        | None                                   |

## Effect
Explodes from the point of origin, inflicting damage to all creatures in the range of action. The explosion ignores any minor covers but cannot pass through significant solid obstacles.
