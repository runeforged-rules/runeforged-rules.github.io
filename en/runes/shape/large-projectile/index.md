# **Large Projectile**

A massive projectile that applies its effects to surrounding creatures during flight.

| **Primary Targets**   | First object hit                     |
| **Secondary Targets** | Creatures within 1 meter of the projectile |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 200 mana                                  |
| **Defense Mod.**        | DEX                                       |

## Effect
The projectile travels in a straight line and hits the first object that intersects its trajectory. While in flight, all creatures within 1 meter of its trajectory are considered secondary targets and suffer the projectile's effects. If it exceeds maximum range without hitting a primary target, it dissipates without producing further effects.
