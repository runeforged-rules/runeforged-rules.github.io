# **Corruption**

Transforms mana into malevolent energy that damages the organism and corrupts the targets' mana, causing debilitating effects.  
Possession and use of this rune are **prohibited**.

| **Damage Type**      | Magic                                    |
| **Damage per LV**       | 1                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 900 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation manifests as dark and corrupting energy, inflicting magic damage and causing mana corruption in hit creatures.  
- **Primary Targets**: Suffer magic damage and corruption levels equal to the evocation level.
- **Secondary Targets**: Suffer magic damage and corruption levels equal to half the evocation level (round down).
