# **Death**

Transforms mana into unstoppable and lethal energy, capable of inflicting devastating damage and definitively ending the targets' lives.  
Possession and use of this rune are **prohibited**.

| **Damage Type**      | Magic                                      |
| **Damage per LV**       | 10                                        |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 5000 mana                                 |

## Effect
The evocation manifests as a lethal power that corrodes targets from the inside, destroying vital organs and tissues. The damage inflicted by this rune does not just hit the surface but causes strong internal deterioration that can often be fatal.  
If a target suffers a mortal wound as an effect of this rune, they die immediately.
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 10 magic damage. If they suffer a mortal wound following the damage inflicted by this rune, they die immediately.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 5 magic damage. If they suffer a mortal wound following the damage inflicted by this rune, they die immediately.
