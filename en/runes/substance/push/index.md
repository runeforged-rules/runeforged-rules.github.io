# **Push**

Transforms mana into a wave of kinetic energy, capable of moving targets and inflicting impact damage.

| **Damage Type**      | Impact                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 400 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation assumes the form of an energy wave that pushes away hit targets, inflicting impact damage and moving them.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 2 impact damage. If they fail a CON or ARC saving throw (DC 9 + LV), they are moved by 1 meter for each evocation level. Inanimate objects are moved if their weight is less than (10 * LV) kg.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 impact damage. If they fail a CON or ARC saving throw (DC 9 + LV), they are moved by 1 meter, and suffer no movement if the throw is successful. Inanimate objects are moved if their weight is less than (5 * LV) kg.
