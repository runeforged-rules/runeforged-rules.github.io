# **Weakness**

Transforms mana into debilitating energy that drains the target of their strength and energy, inflicting levels of weakness without inflicting direct damage.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 0                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 500 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation takes the form of a wave of violet gas, which weakens the target and reduces their ability to fight. Although it inflicts no physical damage, the target suffers progressive levels of weakness that can seriously compromise their physical and mental capacities, up to making them faint.
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target suffers 2 levels of weakness. If they pass a CON or ARC saving throw (DC 9 + LV), the weakness levels suffered are halved.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target suffers 1 level of weakness. If they pass a CON or ARC saving throw (DC 9 + LV), the weakness levels suffered are halved.
