# **Crystal**

Transforms mana into a solid and transparent substance like crystal, capable of stunning targets and creating permanent crystalline formations upon contact with solid surfaces.

| **Damage Type**      | Impact                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 700 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation assumes the appearance of a massive crystal, inflicting impact damage and stun levels to hit creatures. If the evocation hits a solid surface, it generates a crystal 20 cm high per LV and with resistance (HP) equal to 5 times the evocation level.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 2 impact damage and 1 level of stun.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 impact damage.
