# **Regeneration**

Transforms arcane mana into a complete and deep healing energy, capable of returning the target to a full state of health and healing critical conditions.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 0                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 2000 mana                                 |

## Effect
The mana transforms into an intense regenerative force, capable of completely restoring the target's health points and removing mortal wounds.  
- **Primary Targets**: The target completely recovers their health points (HP) and, if they possess a mortal wound status, it is removed immediately. For each LV above the first, they heal 1 wound.
- **Secondary Targets**: The target recovers half of their maximum HP.
