# **Ice**

Transforms mana into a freezing energy that coats its targets in a rigid layer of ice.

| **Damage Type**      | Cold                                    |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 400 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation transforms into a cloud of glacial vapor, which instantly cools every surface it deposits on, inflicting cold damage and slowing hit creatures.
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 2 cold damage.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 cold damage.
