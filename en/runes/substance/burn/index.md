# **Fire**

Transforms mana into an extremely flammable substance, capable of catching fire upon contact with air.

| **Damage Type**      | Fire                                     |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 400 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation assumes the appearance of a live flame, setting fire to flammable objects it comes into contact with and inflicting fire damage to hit creatures.  
- **Primary Targets**: Suffer fire damage equal to twice the evocation level.
- **Secondary Targets**: Suffer fire damage equal to the evocation level.
