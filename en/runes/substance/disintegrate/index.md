# **Disintegration**

Transforms arcane mana into extremely destructive energy, capable of completely disintegrating matter, reducing targets and objects to dust.  
Possession and use of this rune are **prohibited**.

| **Damage Type**      | Magic                                    |
| **Damage per LV**       | 100                                       |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 20000 mana                                |

## Effect
The evocation assumes the form of pure disintegrating energy that annihilates anything it touches. The evocation inflicts high-intensity pure damage, erasing targets and materials without leaving a trace.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 100 magic damage. Any creature that suffers a mortal wound because of this rune is completely disintegrated, leaving only dust.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 50 magic damage.
