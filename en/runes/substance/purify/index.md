# **Purification**

Transforms arcane mana into a purifying energy, capable of freeing the target from debilitating effects and corruptions of an arcane nature, returning them to their full physical and mental capacity.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 0                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 600 mana                                  |

## Effect
The mana manifests as a wave of purifying energy that counters and dissolves debilitating effects and corruptions of arcane origin accumulated by the target.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target recovers 2 HP, reduces 2 levels of weakness suffered, and 2 levels of corruption suffered.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target recovers 1 HP and removes 1 level of weakness and 1 level of corruption.
