# **Blade**

Transforms mana into energy sharp as a blade, capable of generating clean cuts and inflicting deep wounds in hit creatures.

| **Damage Type**      | Slashing                                    |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 300 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation takes the form of a sharp energy blade, inflicting slashing damage and causing bleeding in hit creatures.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 2 slashing damage and 1 level of bleeding.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 slashing damage and 1 level of bleeding. If they pass a CON saving throw (DC 9 + LV), the bleeding levels suffered are halved (round down).
