# **Arrow**

Transforms mana into sharp, piercing energy, capable of causing deep wounds to hit targets.

| **Damage Type**      | Piercing                                |
| **Damage per LV**       | 2                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 200 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation takes the form of a sharp energy arrow, inflicting piercing damage and causing bleeding in hit creatures.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 2 piercing damage and 1 level of bleeding. If they pass a CON saving throw (DC 9 + LV), the bleeding levels suffered are halved (round down).
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 piercing damage.
