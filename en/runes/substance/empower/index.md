# **Power**

Transforms the evocation into an invigorating substance that boosts the physical and magical capabilities of the target, improving their performance in combat and arcana.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 0                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 800 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation transforms into an ethereal substance that remains attached to the target, increasing their physical and arcane capabilities. Although it inflicts no damage, the target becomes stronger, faster, and more resistant, improving both physical and magic-related abilities.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target receives 2 levels of empowerment.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, the target receives 1 level of empowerment.
