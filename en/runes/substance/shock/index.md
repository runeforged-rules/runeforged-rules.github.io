# **Lightning**

Transforms mana into concentrated electrical energy, capable of electrocuting targets and spreading rapidly through conductive objects.

| **Damage Type**      | Lightning                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 1                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 200 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation assumes the appearance of an electrical discharge, inflicting lightning damage and applying the shock effect to hit creatures.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they suffer 1 lightning damage.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each two evocation levels, they suffer 1 lightning damage.
