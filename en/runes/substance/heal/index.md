# **Heal**

Transforms mana into a regenerating energy, capable of healing minor wounds.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | None                                   |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 300 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation takes the form of a cloud of healing energy, restoring the HP of hit targets.  
- **Primary Targets**: For each evocation level, they recover 2 HP.
- **Secondary Targets**: For each evocation level, they recover 1 HP.
