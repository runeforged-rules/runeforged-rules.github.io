# **Null**

Transforms mana into chaotic energy that disturbs the form of the mana it comes into contact with.

| **Damage Type**      | None                                   |
| **Damage per LV**       | 0                                         |
| **Base Cost per LV**  | 100 mana                                  |

## Effect
The evocation is visible only as a faint optical distortion, but its effects are nonetheless devastating for any form of arcane energy. The energy released by this rune is so chaotic in nature that it makes the mana it contacts uncontrollable, instantly dissolving it into a state of raw mana.   
- **Primary Targets**: Targets lose control of all arcane cores they possess, and targeted evocations lose LV equal to the LV of this evocation.
- **Secondary Targets**: Targets lose control of all arcane cores they possess, and targeted evocations of level equal to or lower than half the level of this one (round down) are instantly annulled.
