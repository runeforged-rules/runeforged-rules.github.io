# **Sniper**

A Sniper is distinguished by their inexhaustible patience, iron will, and superhuman precision. Following this path is as much a battle against one's enemies as against oneself, and only the most persistent and talented manage to perfect the art. These Snipers are unparalleled in precision and lethality at a distance.

| **Level 3**  | **Aim** Lv.1: If you have a ranged weapon equipped, you can use **1** action to take aim and enter a state of **concentration**. You obtain **+1 PRC** until you move, reload, or are hit.
| **Level 4**  | **Quick Reload**: Reloading any ranged weapon that would cost **2 or more actions** takes **1 fewer action**. Reloading **does not interrupt** **concentration**.
| **Level 5**  | **Steady Hands**: While you have **concentration**, your ranged shots have **doubled range** and **+2** on attack rolls.
| **Level 6**  | **Aim** Lv.2: The bonus obtained during **concentration** becomes **+2 PRC**.
| **Level 7**  | **Lethal Strike**: When you hit a creature with a **precision strike**, it also suffers 3 levels of bleeding.
| **Level 8**  | **Deep Concentration**: You can move up to **half your movement** per action without breaking **concentration**, and when you suffer damage, you lose **concentration** only if you fail a **CON saving throw (DC 10)**.
| **Level 9**  | **Aim** Lv.3: The bonus obtained during **concentration** becomes **+3 PRC**.
| **Level 10**  | **Headhunter**: Your **precision strikes** while you have **concentration** are considered normal strikes regarding the calculation of the target's defenses, and if they hit the head, they are automatically critical.
