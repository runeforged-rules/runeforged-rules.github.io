# **Sharpshooter**

The discipline of the Sharpshooter requires unparalleled precision and reaction speed.  
Born with the invention of the crossbow, it remained relatively unknown until it was adopted en masse by the soldiers of the Machian Empire after the advent of the rifle as a weapon of war. As a result of this military revolution, Sharpshooter techniques spread across the continent, becoming extremely popular in both military and recreational fields. However, despite the discipline's recent popularity, only a few practitioners have the skills to reach the level necessary to unlock its true potential.

| **Level 1**  | **Lethal Precision**: Add your **PRC** to the damage dealt by attacks with ranged weapons.
| **Level 2**  | **Repositioning**: As part of an attack of opportunity, you can move a distance equal to **half** your movement.
| **Level 3 - 10**  | **Path Choice**: [Sniper](./sniper) / [Gunslinger](./gunslinger).
