# **Gunslinger**

The perfect Sharpshooter is a machine of mechanical precision, who takes aim and continues firing until the target has no way out. The Gunslinger starts from these principles to refine agility, versatility, and dexterity with weapons. When faced with an expert Gunslinger, there is no cover that can protect from the incoming rain of lead.

| **Level 3**  | **Dual Wielding** Lv.1: **Once** per turn, while using **two single-handed ranged weapons**, you can attack with both in the same attack action.
| **Level 4**  | **Double Reload**: While using two single-handed ranged weapons, the reload action **triggers the reload for both**, using the duration of the one that **costs more actions**.
| **Level 5**  | **Agile Gunslinger**: While using single-handed ranged weapons, you can **move and attack** in the same action **without suffering attack roll penalties**.
| **Level 6**  | **Dual Wielding** Lv.2: Can be used **twice** per turn.
| **Level 7**  | **Sleight of Hand**: You can reload once per turn as a free action.
| **Level 8**  | **Shot Sequence**: For every attack **that hits the target**, you obtain **+1 PRC** until the end of the turn.
| **Level 9**  | **Dual Wielding** Lv.3: Can be used **three** times per turn.
| **Level 10**  | **Double Shot**: If you hit a creature twice **in the same attack action**, they suffer **1** level of stun.
