# **Elementalist**

The Elementalist is an Arcanist who chooses to dedicate themselves to perfecting a single discipline, rather than trying to explore them all and risking remaining in mediocrity.  
Fueled by a passion for elemental substance runes, the Elementalist sets the single goal of perfecting their evocation.

# **Elemental Runes**

* [**Fire (Burn)**][fo]
* [**Ice (Freeze)**][gh]
* [**Crystal (Crystalize)**][cr]
* [**Lightning (Shock)**][fu]

| **Level 3** | **Elemental Overload** Lv.1: The **DC** for the saving throw of **elemental runes** is increased by **2**.
| **Level 6** | **Runic Specialization**: Innately evoked elemental runes obtain an additional effect:<br><br>[**_Fire (Burn)_**][fo]: The **damage per level** of the rune is increased by **+1**.<br><br>[**_Ice (Freeze)_**][gh]: The **frostbite** inflicted applies **3 cold damage** at the start of the enemy's turn, instead of **1**.<br><br>[**_Crystal (Crystalize)_**][cr]: The base cost per LV of the rune is reduced to **500 MP per LV**.<br><br>[**_Lightning (Shock)_**][fu]: The shock levels applied by this rune are **doubled**.
| **Level 8** | **Elemental Overload** Lv.2: The **DC** for the saving throw of **elemental runes** is increased by **4**, instead of 2.
| **Level 10** | **Perfect Evocation**: Innately evoked **elemental runes** are considered **Grade 5**.
