# **Sorcerer**

A Sorcerer dedicates their life to deepening their connection with mana. They perceive and shape it as if it were an extension of their own body. For a Sorcerer, there is no greater satisfaction than giving life to their imagination and inventing spells never seen before, without being limited by the rigidity of runes regarding shape and effects, which can be molded to their liking. In the hands of an expert Sorcerer, mana seems to move as if it had a life of its own.

| **Level 3** | **Free Evocation** Lv.1: Once per combat, you can use an action to choose **one** or **two** substance runes you know how to evoke innately. Evoke these runes with shape and targets **at will**. The shape uses **mana per LV** equal to 500 base MP + **250 MP for each targeted creature**, and is limited by significant obstacles and a maximum distance of **10** meters from the evoker.
| **Level 6** | **Free Evocation** Lv.2: Usable **twice** per combat. The shape cost per LV is reduced to **300 + 200** for each target, and the maximum distance from the evoker is extended to **15** meters.
| **Level 8** | **Arcane Attunement**: You can use **1** action to channel the mana present around you, regenerating mana equal to **half of your passive regeneration**. After using this action, you cannot use it again for **1 hour**.
| **Level 10** | **Arcane Mastery**: Free Evocation can be used **once per turn** and the maximum distance of the shape is extended to **20** meters. When you have negative mana, the mana obtained is **no longer halved**, and you suffer **one level less** of mana affliction.
