# **Arcanist**

The Arcanist is an evoker who prefers to use instinct and their own creativity to evoke [arcane cores](/en/runes/arcane-core), instead of relying exclusively on runes. To be an Arcanist, it is necessary—through talent or hard practice—to become an **innate evoker** capable of manifesting the effects of various runes without them being physically present. This is made possible by a great mastery of the [arcane core](/en/runes/arcane-core) generation process, which enables the user to manifest arcane cores with properties at will, potentially **mimicking runes** of any type.

| **Level 1**  | **Innate Evoker**: Choose a substance rune from [**Fire (Burn)**][fo], [**Ice (Freeze)**][gh], [**Crystal (Crystalize)**][cr], or [**Lightning (Shock)**][fu]. You are capable of manifesting this rune innately, preceded by the [**Projectile**][pr] rune. Both the chosen substance rune and the Projectile rune are **Grade 0**. The maximum LV of the innate evocation is equal to your ARC score.
| **Level 2**  | **Arcane Intuition** Lv.1: Choose a substance rune from **those not selected at level 1** and [**Push**](/en/runes/substance/push/), [**Arrow (Pierce)**](/en/runes/substance/pierce/), or [**Blade (Sever)**](/en/runes/substance/sever/). You are also capable of manifesting this rune innately, preceded by the [**Projectile**][pr] shape rune. All innately manifested runes are **Grade 1**.
| **Level 3**  | **Path Choice**: [Elementalist][elem] / [Sorcerer][sorc].
| **Level 4**  | **Flash of Inspiration**: Once per rest, you can receive a flash of inspiration, allowing you to modify an arcane core you are creating with the [**Join**](/en/runes/control/join/) rune, followed by one of the other shapes you know. Only the [**Join**](/en/runes/control/join/) rune in this core **costs 0 MP**.
| **Level 5**  | **Arcane Intuition** Lv.2: Choose two substance runes from those not yet selected and [**Corruption**](/en/runes/substance/corrupt/), [**Power (Empower)**](/en/runes/substance/empower/), or [**Damage (Kill)**](/en/runes/substance/kill/). You are also capable of manifesting these runes innately. All innately manifested runes are preceded by the [**Projectile**][pr] or [**Piercing Projectile**][ppr] shape rune. All innately manifested runes are **Grade 2**.
| **Level 6**  | **Path Ability**: [Elementalist][elem] / [Sorcerer][sorc].
| **Level 7**  | **Arcane Intuition** Lv.3: Choose two substance runes from those not yet selected; they can be manifested innately. All innately manifested runes are preceded by one of the following shape runes: [**Projectile**][pr] / [**Piercing Projectile**][ppr] / [**Ray**][ra]. All innately manifested runes are **Grade 3**.
| **Level 8**  | **Path Ability**: [Elementalist][elem] / [Sorcerer][sorc].
| **Level 9**  | **Arcane Intuition** Lv.4: Choose two substance runes from those not yet selected and [**Teleport**](/en/runes/substance/teleport/), [**Death (Kill)**](/en/runes/substance/kill/), or [**Acid**](/en/runes/substance/acid). You are also capable of manifesting these runes innately. All innately manifested runes are **Grade 4**.
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Elementalist][elem] / [Sorcerer][sorc].
