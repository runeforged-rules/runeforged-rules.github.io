# **Alchemist**

| **Level 2**  | **Infusion**: You are capable of infusing an [evocation](/en/runes/evocation) within a mana container **of any type**. When the container's mana is released, it **automatically activates** the infused evocation.|
| **Level 6**  | **Transmutation**: You are capable of spending **10000 mana** to temporarily transform 1 kg of metal into orichalcum for **1 day**. The material returns to its original state if transmutation is not reapplied during this period. If the material undergoes **5** consecutive transmutations, it becomes orichalcum **permanently**. You can also spend **1000 mana** to permanently transform 1 kg of **orichalcum into ordinary metal**.|
| **Level 10**  | **Mutation**: You are able to transform your body at will and can use 1 action to become **mutated**. Mutation **increases maximum HP** by your **AFF** score, your maximum mana per [arcane core](/en/runes/arcane-core) **doubles**, and wounds can be healed by spending **5000 mana**. Mutation is reversible through a meditation process for the duration of **1 hour**. The process is interrupted by any action performed or damage received. For every day spent **mutated**, the meditation process must last **1 hour longer**.|
