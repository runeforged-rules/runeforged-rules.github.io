# **Runemaster**

| **Level 2**  | **Relaxed Writing**: You are capable of writing runes **during** a short rest or a long rest, if the writing time **does not exceed half** of the rest time.|
| **Level 6**  | **Calligraphy**: Writing **Grade 1** runes in combat lasts **1 action**, and writing **Grade 2** runes lasts **1 turn**. Writing runes during a long rest takes **half the time**. |
| **Level 10**  | **Perfection**: You are able to improve written **Grade 4** runes into **Grade 5** runes, taking 5 hours of time.|
