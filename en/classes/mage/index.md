# **Mage**

Description of the Mage

| **Level 1** | **Runic Knowledge**: You know and can identify **all non-forbidden runes** and **one forbidden rune** of your choice, and you are capable of inscribing them **up to Grade 2**. Writing runes on paper takes a defined amount of time in the [**writing times table**](#writing-times-on-paper). You can only write runes **outside of combat**.|
| **Level 2** | **Path Choice**: [Alchemist](./alchemist) / [Runemaster](./runemaster).|
| **Level 3** | **Fast Writing**: You can write runes even **during combat**.|
| **Level 4** | **Runic Study**: You are capable of writing runes up to **Grade 3**.|
| **Level 5** | **Arcane Preparation** Lv.1: You can use **1 action** to generate a stable [arcane core](/en/runes/arcane-core). The stable core and arcane cores can be maintained simultaneously, but the stable core dissipates after **1 turn** or if another stable core is created.|
| **Level 6** | **Path Ability**: [Alchemist](./alchemist) / [Runemaster](./runemaster).|
| **Level 7** | **Runic Mastery**: You are capable of writing runes up to **Grade 4**.|
| **Level 8** | **Arcane Battery**: Your body is able to absorb and retain more mana than normal, increasing your maximum mana by **1000 per Mage level**.|
| **Level 9** | **Arcane Preparation** Lv.2: Up to **two stable cores** can be maintained simultaneously, and they dissipate after **1 minute**.|
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Alchemist](./alchemist) / [Runemaster](./runemaster).|
