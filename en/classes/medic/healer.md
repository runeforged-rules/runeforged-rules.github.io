# **Healer**

| **Level 2**  | **Purification**: Once per turn, as an action, you can spend **1000 MP** to remove **all levels** of a [condition](/en/attributes/status-conditions) possessed by yourself or a creature you can touch.|
| **Level 6**  | **Continuous Regeneration**: At the start of each of your turns, you regenerate **HP equal to your ARC score**. Every creature targeted by **Arcane Healing** receives this effect for **2 turns**.|
| **Level 10**  | **Arcane Reconstitution**: Once per turn, as an action during your turn, you can use **1000 MP** to heal **1 wound** on yourself or a creature you can touch. If the creature is afflicted by a [Mortal Wound](/en/attributes/health), the mana cost increases to **1500 MP**, but it also removes the Mortal Wound condition from the targeted creature.|
