# **Surgeon**

| **Level 2**  | **Tools of the Trade**: Using a tool to heal HP or wounds has its duration **reduced by 1 action**, down to a minimum duration of 1 action. The damage dealt by your **precision strikes** increases by a number equal to your **PRC** score.|
| **Level 6**  | **Emergency Operation**: If in possession of surgeon tools, once per long rest, while performing the rest, you can **heal 1 wound** from yourself or an ally for **500 MP**.|
| **Level 10**  | **Infallible Procedure**: If in possession of surgeon tools, you can **heal all wounds** of an ally by spending **one hour and 500 MP per wound**. Both you and the ally must remain undisturbed at the operation site for the entire duration.|
