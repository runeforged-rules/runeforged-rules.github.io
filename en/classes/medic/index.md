# **Medic**

Description of the Medic

| **Level 1** | **Arcane Healing**: As an action during your turn, you can use your mana to heal yourself or a creature you can touch, at a cost of **100 MP per 1 HP** healed, up to a maximum amount of HP equal to **double your Medic level**.|
| **Level 2** | **Path Choice**: [Surgeon](./surgeon) / [Healer](./healer).|
| **Level 3** | **Anatomical Studies** Lv.1: When you hit a human with a precision strike, you also inflict **1 level of bleeding**. When you use a tool to regenerate HP, you can add your **PRC** modifier to the total HP healed.|
| **Level 4** | **First Aid**: As an action during your turn, you can spend **500 MP** to heal yourself or a creature you can touch from the [Mortal Wound](/en/attributes/health) state.|
| **Level 5** | **Painkiller**: As an action during your turn, you can use **500 MP** to apply a painkiller effect to yourself or a creature you can touch. For **1 minute**, the target can drop to **-5 HP** before suffering a wound. |
| **Level 6** | **Path Ability**: [Surgeon](./surgeon) / [Healer](./healer).|
| **Level 7** | **Anatomical Studies** Lv.2: When you hit a human with a precision strike **to the head** that would inflict a wound, their [death saving throw](/en/attributes/health#death-saving-throw) for that wound **automatically fails**. You always succeed on **PRC** or **ARC** checks when using a tool to heal a wound. |
| **Level 8** | **Energizing Healing**: Healing a creature with **Arcane Healing** allows them to perform one extra action during their next turn.|
| **Level 9** | **Resuscitation**: Once per day, using two actions, you can spend 10000 MP to resuscitate a creature that has been dead for less than a minute. |
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Surgeon](./surgeon) / [Healer](./healer).|
