# **Commander**

The discipline of the Commander arises from an alternative vision of the Guardian, which finds its origins among the warrior tribes of the Miden desert. While maintaining the fundamental principles of training and team tactics, it adds that barbaric ferocity to the fighting style that distinguishes desert warriors. An expert Commander dedicates themselves equally to the perfection of offensive and defensive techniques, making them a formidable enemy for any creature that opposes them.

| **Level 2** | **Shield Bash**: Once per turn, if you have a **shield** equipped and hit a target with a melee weapon, you can add a guaranteed shield bash to the attack, adding **1d6 + STR** impact damage to the damage dealt.
| **Level 4** | **Center of Attention**: When you hit a hostile creature, you can use an action to **draw its attention**. The creature must perform a **CON** saving throw (**DC 10 + Guardian level**); if they fail, they are forced to target only the Guardian with their attacks for **3** turns.
| **Level 6** | **Shield Bash** Lv.2: The damage inflicted by the shield bash increases to **2d6 + STR**.
| **Level 8** | **Retaliation**: The first attack against a creature that has dealt damage to you has **advantage** on the attack roll. This privilege repeats every time you suffer damage from a creature.
| **Level 10** | **Shield Bash** Lv.3: The creature hit by the shield receives **3** levels of **stun**.
