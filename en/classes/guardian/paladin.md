# **Paladin**

Over the centuries, the honor guards of the King of Dubris have perfected the martial techniques of the Guardian, refining team defense in particular, thus creating an impenetrable formation that has become the model for all elite units on the continent. The company of an expert Paladin is often a guarantee that all allies will return safe and sound. Wherever they are, the Paladin will be the first line of defense for the group, and the last to fall.

| **Level 2** | **Protection**: Once per turn, if an **allied** creature is targeted by an attack or spell, you can move toward them for a distance up to your movement. If you manage to reach the position of the targeted ally, you can choose to **become the new target** of the attack.
| **Level 4** | **Valor**: Every time you are targeted by an attack, you accumulate 1 charge of **Valor**. You can spend **Valor** charges when you declare an attack to obtain **+1** to the attack roll and damage for each charge used. More than one **Valor** charge can be used per attack, up to a maximum equal to your Guardian level.<br>With 10 or more **Valor** charges: if an attack would mortally wound you, consume all **Valor** charges to automatically pass the death saving throw. **Valor** charges return to **0** at the end of combat.
| **Level 6** | **Protection** Lv.2: Can be used **twice** per turn, with a shared total distance limit. The movement performed with this ability in a turn cannot exceed your movement statistic.
| **Level 8** | **Impenetrable Honor**: When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you obtain 1 charge of **Valor**. For every **5** **Valor** charges possessed, you obtain **+1 AC**, up to a maximum of **+3 AC** at **15** charges.
| **Level 10** | **Protection** Lv.3: Has **no limit** on uses, but the shared total distance limit for uses per turn remains equal to your movement.
