# **Guardian**

Born as a martial discipline for the royal guard of Dubris, it gradually spread throughout the realm until it became officially recognized across the continent. Practitioners are subjected to a strict training regime, as well as countless trials of physical and mental endurance. Becoming a Guardian means dedicating your life to the perfection of physical form, the defense of the kingdom, and the protection of the weak.

| **Level 1**  | **Active Parry**: Use a reaction to deflect an attack targeting you. The attacker suffers **disadvantage** on the attack roll and, if it hits, the damage taken is reduced by a number equal to your Guardian level (down to a minimum of 1). This ability can be used after the attacker has rolled to hit, forcing them to roll again and use the lower result, but it **cannot** be used after the dice roll for damage calculation.
| **Level 2**  | **Path Choice**: [Paladin](./paladin) / [Commander](./commander).
| **Level 3**  | **Defensive Stance**: Once per turn, if you have a shield equipped, you can use an action to prepare to defend, obtaining **+2 AC** until the start of your next turn.
| **Level 4**  | **Path Ability**: [Paladin](./paladin) / [Commander](./commander).
| **Level 5**  | **Iron Will**: When the Guardian is hit by a hostile creature, they immediately recover their reaction if it has already been used.
| **Level 6**  | **Path Ability**: [Paladin](./paladin) / [Commander](./commander).
| **Level 7**  | **Counterattack**: If a hostile creature within proximity of the Guardian (**1.5m**) attacks an allied creature (including themselves), the Guardian can use their reaction to declare an attack of opportunity against the attacking creature.
| **Level 8**  | **Path Ability**: [Paladin](./paladin) / [Commander](./commander).
| **Level 9**  | **Human Wall**: In combat, if the Guardian is standing, they are considered an impassable wall for hostile creatures (a cylinder 1.5 meters in diameter, 2 meters high); as such, it is not possible to see or target creatures hidden behind them. When the Guardian is afflicted by a **Mortal Wound**: they do not fall to the ground, they have advantage on the **death saving throw**, and can move half their movement per action.
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Paladin](./paladin) / [Commander](./commander).
