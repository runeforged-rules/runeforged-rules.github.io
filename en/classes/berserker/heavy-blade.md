# **Shattering Strike**

The expert Berserker analyzes their opponent before the battle even begins. Once weaknesses are identified, a single hit is necessary to guarantee victory. Your victims will fall under the weight of your blade.

| **Level 3** | **Brutal Preparation**: You can use an action to prepare to strike, obtaining **+3** to the attack roll and damage inflicted by your next attacks this turn.
| **Level 5** | **Aimed Strike**: The first attack roll of the turn after Brutal Preparation is made with advantage.
| **Level 6** | **Brutal Preparation** Lv.2: You obtain **+5** to the roll and damage of next attacks this turn, and enemies hit this turn suffer a level of **stun**.
| **Level 9** | **Brutal Preparation** Lv.3: You obtain **+7** to the roll and damage of next attacks this turn, and enemies hit this turn suffer a level of both **stun** and **bleeding**.
| **Level 10** | **Guillotine**: Once per rest, you can choose to make one of your attacks a lethal blow. The lethal blow has **advantage** on the attack roll and, if it hits, is automatically a **critical** hit.
