# **Steel Storm**

A true Berserker is unstoppable. For the warriors of Miden, leaving the enemy even a single moment of respite is often a fatal mistake. Your attacks will be a steel storm from which your opponents cannot escape.

| **Level 3** | **Flurry**: Once per turn, if you **do not** have a two-handed weapon equipped, you can attack twice in the attack action.
| **Level 5** | **Stunning Strikes**: When you hit a target, your attack rolls against the same target obtain **+1** until the end of the turn. This effect is applied for each attack performed successfully. 
| **Level 6** | **Flurry** Lv.2: **Flurry** allows you to attack **three** times in the attack action.
| **Level 9** | **Flurry** Lv.3: **Flurry** can be used twice per turn.
| **Level 10** | **Grand Finale**: Once per turn, you can make a chosen attack a final blow. This attack has **advantage** on the attack roll and inflicts **+1** damage for each of your attacks that hit a target this turn. This attack **cannot** be a precision strike.
