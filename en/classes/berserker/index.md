# **Berserker**

The discipline of the Berserker originates from the deserts of Miden, where fighters train from a young age to use their mana as protective energy to reinforce their body. The result of their training and the brutal physical trials they undergo is a warrior who does not hesitate to throw themselves into the fray, but who knows how to use their mana reserves to heal themselves and stay in battle longer.

## Abilities by Level:

| **Level 1**  | **Vital Mana**: When hit by an attack or evocation, you can sacrifice mana to mitigate the damage taken at a cost of **200 mana per HP**, up to a maximum amount of damage equal to **double** your **ARC** score.
| **Level 2**  | **Running Attack**: Once per turn, you can perform an attack during the dash action, but this attack **cannot** be a precision strike.
| **Level 3**  | **Path Choice**: [Steel Storm](./steel-storm) / [Shattering Strike](./heavy-blade).
| **Level 4**  | **Terrifying Presence**: Enemies within **1.5** meters of the Berserker have **disadvantage** on attack rolls with ranged attacks.
| **Level 5**  | **Path Ability**: [Steel Storm](./steel-storm) / [Shattering Strike](./heavy-blade).
| **Level 6**  | Level of the chosen path ability **+ 1**
| **Level 7**  | **Unstoppable**: When you would suffer the [**mortal wound**](/en/attributes/health#mortal-wound) state, you can decide to spend **1000 mana** to negate it.
| **Level 8**  | **Expert Heal**: You can use an action to consume **2000 mana** and heal **1 wound** suffered.
| **Level 9**  | Level of the chosen path ability **+ 1**
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Steel Storm](./steel-storm) / [Shattering Strike](./heavy-blade).
