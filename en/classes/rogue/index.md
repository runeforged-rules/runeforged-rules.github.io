# **Rogue**

Description of the Rogue

| **Level 1** | **Lightning Dash Rune** Lv.1: You possess a rune (in the form of an inscribed object, a tattoo, or similar) that has the effect of making you **dash instantly** in the direction of use up to a distance of **10 meters**. The dash bypasses creatures and minor obstacles but ends when you come into contact with a significant obstacle or in the last allowable position (occupying the same space as a creature or minor obstacle is not allowed). Using the rune consumes **10 MP** per meter, costs **1 action**, and leaves a glowing trail (visible for a few seconds) in the space traversed. Creatures bypassed during the dash suffer a level of [stun](/en/attributes/status-conditions). |
| **Level 2** | **Path Choice**: [Assassin](./assassin) / [Trickster](./trickster).|
| **Level 3** | **Unexpected Strike**: When you hit an enemy from behind, if the hit is not **critical**, it becomes one; if it already was, add your **PRC** score to the damage dealt. An enemy is considered "from behind" if they have an enemy within **1 meter** of them, if you have **bypassed** them with the Lightning Dash Rune this turn, or if they are **unaware** of your presence. |
| **Level 4** | **Path Ability**: [Assassin](./assassin) / [Trickster](./trickster).|
| **Level 5** | **Lightning Dash Rune** Lv.2: The Lightning Dash Rune can reach a distance of 20 meters, and the mana cost is reduced to **5 MP** per meter. You can overload the rune by using **20 MP** per meter to dash as a free action on your turn; this dash can bypass significant obstacles and does not leave a visible trail, but does not inflict stun on traversed creatures. |
| **Level 6** | **Path Ability**: [Assassin](./assassin) / [Trickster](./trickster).|
| **Level 7** | **Precise Dodge**: When you perform the dodge reaction, reduce the attacker's hit result by a number equal to your DEX modifier + your PRC modifier. |
| **Level 8** | **Path Ability**: [Assassin](./assassin) / [Trickster](./trickster).|
| **Level 9** | **Lightning Dash Rune** Lv.3: The Lightning Dash Rune can reach a distance of 30 meters, and you can pay double the **MP** to be able to attack a creature you bypass. |
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Assassin](./assassin) / [Trickster](./trickster).|
