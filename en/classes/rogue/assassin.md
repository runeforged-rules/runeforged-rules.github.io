# **Assassin**

| **Level 2**  | **Identify Weakness** Lv.1: Once per turn, as an action, you can identify a weakness on an enemy you can see. A random body part receives a weakness. When you hit an enemy using a precision strike, remove all weaknesses on the hit part, dealing 1d4 additional pure damage for each weakness, or 2d4 per weakness if the hit is critical. Each creature can have a maximum of 2 weaknesses. |
| **Level 4**  | **Combat Analysis**: When you are attacked by an enemy on which at least one weakness is identified, you can use a reaction to give disadvantage to the attack roll or obtain advantage on a saving throw for this attack.  |
| **Level 6**  | **Identify Weakness** Lv.2: When you hit a part with weaknesses using a precision strike, you deal 1d6 additional pure damage, or 2d6 if the hit is critical. Each creature can have a maximum of 3 weaknesses. |
| **Level 8**  | **Lethal Technique**: When an enemy suffers a wound from damage dealt by an attack that hit a weakness, the death saving throw is made with disadvantage. |
| **Level 10**  | **Identify Weakness** Lv.3: Once per turn, you can identify a weakness as a free action. When you hit a part with weaknesses using a precision strike, you deal 1d8 additional pure damage and apply 4 levels of bleeding, or 2d8 pure damage and 8 levels of bleeding if the hit is critical. |
