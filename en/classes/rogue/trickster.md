# **Trickster**

| **Level 2**  | **Arcane Disturbance** Lv.1: Once per turn, when you use the Lightning Dash Rune to bypass an enemy, you can inflict a penalty of -1 to their STR, PRC, and ARC scores for one turn in addition to the stun level. |
| **Level 4**  | **Siphon**: When you deal damage to an enemy with a precision strike using a melee weapon or when you bypass them with the Lightning Dash Rune, you can attempt to steal mana from the target. Roll a d20 + PRC if attacking, or a d20 + ARC if dashing, and steal MP from the target creature equal to X% of their current mana, where X is the result of the roll. |
| **Level 6**  | **Arcane Disturbance** Lv.2: Once per turn, when you use the Lightning Dash Rune to bypass an enemy, you can inflict a penalty of -2 to their STR, PRC, and ARC scores for one turn in addition to the stun level. |
| **Level 8**  | **Transfusion**: When you use the Lightning Dash Rune to bypass an enemy, you can spend 1000 MP to release a flashbang effect, applying 2 levels of stun and inflicting arcane stun on the target until your next turn. All attacks targeting a creature afflicted by arcane stun obtain advantage on the attack roll. |
| **Level 10**  | **Lightning Dodge**: As a reaction in response to an attack, you can activate the Lightning Dash Rune to change position. If after the dash you are still a valid target for the attack, the attack roll suffers disadvantage; otherwise, the attack fails. |
