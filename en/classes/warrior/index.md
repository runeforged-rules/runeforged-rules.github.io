# **Warrior**

Description of the Warrior

| **Level 1** | **Martial Proficiency**: All weapons you use receive the maximum of your proficiency bonus.|
| **Level 2** | **Path Choice**: [Duelist](./duelist) / [Executioner](./executioner).|
| **Level 3** | **Double Attack**: Once per turn, you can attack twice during the attack action.|
| **Level 4** | **Latent Vigor**: Twice per rest, you can obtain an extra action at the end of your turn. Maximum 4 actions per turn. |
| **Level 5** | **Armor Proficiency**: The weight of armor you wear is considered of the lower category. (Heavy -> Medium, Medium -> Light, Light -> None)|
| **Level 6** | **Path Ability**: [Duelist](./duelist) / [Executioner](./executioner).|
| **Level 7** | **Triple Attack**: Once per turn, you can attack three times during the attack action.|
| **Level 8** | **Endure Pain**: Once per rest, when you suffer a wound and would fail the death saving throw, it succeeds automatically instead. |
| **Level 9** | **War Master**: While using a melee weapon, you can add your CON score to your STR score; while using a ranged weapon, you can add your DEX score to your PRC score. |
| **Level 10** | **Path Ability**: [Duelist](./duelist) / [Executioner](./executioner).|
