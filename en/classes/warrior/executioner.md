# **Executioner**

| **Level 2**  | **Lethal Strike**: When you inflict a wound on a creature, the death saving throw for the inflicted wound receives -1 to the result. | 
| **Level 6**  | **Deep Wounds**: When you inflict damage on a creature, you also inflict 1 level of [severe wound](/en/attributes/status-conditions). |
| **Level 10**  | **Finishing Blow**: Using 1 action during your turn, you can kill a creature afflicted by a mortal wound that you are able to attack. |
