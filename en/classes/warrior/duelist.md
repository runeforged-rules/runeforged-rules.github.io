# **Duelist**

| **Level 2**  | **En garde!**: You can use an action to challenge a creature that can see you. You have **+2 to the roll and advantage** on attack rolls for **precision strikes** against the challenged creature, and all creatures that are **not** challenged have advantage on attack rolls against you. | 
| **Level 6**  | **Superior Counterattack**: When you perform the parry action, you have advantage on the parry roll. You are also able to parry attacks made with ranged weapons (PRC attribute). Parrying a ranged attack does not end the attacker's turn, and missing the parry does not make the attack critical.|
| **Level 10**  | **Instant Strikes**: Your enemies cannot use reactions in response to your attacks, and their rolls for defensive actions like parry, dodge, or block have disadvantage.|
