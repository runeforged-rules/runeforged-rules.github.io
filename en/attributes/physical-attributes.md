# **Physical Attributes**

## **Strength (STR)**
Determines physical force and muscular power. The score is added to attack rolls and damage with strength weapons, to saving throws regarding strength, to success rolls for brute force-based checks, and increases the maximum weight that can be carried.

## **Precision (PRC)**
Determines proficiency with precision tools and aim with ranged weapons. The score is added to attack rolls of ranged weapons, to rolls regarding precise movements, and lowers the critical threshold of inflicted attacks (a hit is critical if the d20 result + PRC is greater than 20).

## **Dexterity (DEX)**
Determines agility of movement and speed. Contributes to the character's Armor Class (AC), is used in saving throws requiring evasive speed, and in checks based on reflex readiness or rapid movements. The score is further added to the Local Armor (LA) of each body part and helps defend against precision strikes.

## **Constitution (CON)**
Determines physical resistance against attacks and diseases. Contributes to the character's Armor Class (AC) and is used in saving throws to endure or resist an attack. The score determines the character's maximum health points and resistance to suffering mortal wounds.

## **Arcane (ARC)**
Determines skill and efficiency in mana control. The score indicates how much mana can be used per evocation and is used in saving throws regarding the use or manipulation of mana.

## **Mana Affinity (AFF)**
Determines physical adaptation to the presence of mana. The score contributes to the maximum capacity and passive absorption of mana. It is used for rolls regarding the ability to absorb or perceive surrounding mana, and determines the character's maximum mana points.

When a character levels up, they can increase a physical attribute of their choice by **1 point**, with the restriction of **not** being able to increase the **same attribute chosen** at the **previous** level.
