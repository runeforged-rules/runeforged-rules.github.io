# **Effects and Conditions**

|**Burn<br>1 level per fire damage taken** | At the start of their turn, the afflicted creature takes fire damage equal to the burn level and reduces their burn level by 5.<br>After taking damage, the creature can decide to use 1 action to extinguish the flames, halving the burn level, or zeroing it if less than 5.|
|**Frostbite<br>1 level per cold damage taken**| At the start of their turn, the afflicted creature takes 1 cold damage. For their turn, they have -1 meter of movement for each frostbite level.<br>When movement is reduced to 0, each additional frostbite level inflicts 1 cold damage.<br>The frostbite level decreases by 2 at the end of their turn.|
|**Shock<br>1 level per lightning damage taken**| A creature afflicted by shock takes 1 lightning damage every time they use a reaction.<br>At the start of their turn, if the creature is afflicted by at least 5 shock levels, the creature takes lightning damage equal to the shock level and loses 1 action.<br>The shock level returns to 0 at the end of their turn.|
|**Stun**| A creature afflicted by stun has disadvantage on attack rolls with ranged weapons.<br>At the start of their turn, for every 3 stun levels suffered, the afflicted creature loses 1 action.<br>The stun level returns to 0 at the end of their turn.|
|**Bleeding**| At the start of their turn, the afflicted creature takes irreducible damage equal to the bleeding level.<br>The bleeding level decreases by 1 at the end of their turn.<br>When the creature afflicted by bleeding is healed, the bleeding level is reduced by the same number of HP healed.|
|**Deafness**| The afflicted creature cannot perceive through hearing until the end of the condition.<br>The condition ends at the end of the afflicted creature's second turn.|
|**Severe Wound**| The afflicted creature only receives half of the healed health points. The severe wound level is reduced by 1 for each regenerated health point.| 
|**Mortal Wound**| The afflicted creature is on the brink of death and can perform only 1 action per turn, and their movement is reduced to 0.<br>At the end of their turn, the creature must pass a [**death saving throw**](./health#death-saving-throw) to avoid dying.|
|**Weakness**| The creature loses 1 meter of movement for each level of weakness and has disadvantage on attack rolls with melee weapons. Weakness levels return to 0 at the end of their turn.|
|**Mana Loss**| For every 1000 negative MP, a creature is afflicted by 1 level of mana loss:<br>For each level, the afflicted creature loses 3 maximum HP and has -1 in ARC and AFF.|
|**Corruption**| The creature is afflicted by corrosive energy, reducing maximum HP by 1 for each corruption level. Maximum HP cannot be reduced to zero by the effect of corruption. The afflicted creature can use 1 action to expel the malevolent energy, rolling a d4 + ARC and reducing their corruption by levels equal to the result obtained.|
|**Empowerment**| The creature is empowered by arcane energy. They obtain +1 to all rolls based on [physical attributes](./physical-attributes).<br>At the end of their turn, the empowerment level decreases by 1.|
