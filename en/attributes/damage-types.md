# **Damage Types**

Physical:
- **Impact**: Damage taken from hits with blunt weapons, collisions with heavy objects, and fall damage.
- **Slashing**: Damage taken from sharp weapons, claws, or blades.
- **Piercing**: Damage taken from arrows, sword thrusts, or spear strikes.

Elemental:
- **Fire**: Damage inflicted by flames, extreme heat, or burns.
