# **Bloodline**

## **Human**
The common human being. Possesses low mana affinity and regular physical attributes. Cannot retain mana for long in their body but can still channel it, though with reduced effectiveness.

## **Pureblood** 
Direct descendant of arcane families. Endowed with very high mana affinity, they are capable of controlling and retaining it to the point of being able to passively absorb it from the air, but due to genetic defects, their physique is relatively weak.

## **Half-blood**
Born from a pureblood and a common human, they possess moderate mana affinity that allows them to retain and channel mana in their body easily, but due to a genetic defect, their organism needs to consume a constant dose of mana to survive.

## **Mana-less**
Pure descendant of an ancient lineage. Uncontaminated by mana, they are incapable of channeling mana of any type. Physical attributes are average.
