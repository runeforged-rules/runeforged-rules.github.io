# **Personal Attributes**

## **Deception**
Determines the ability to lie and go unnoticed.

## **Presence**
Determines the ability to intimidate and capture attention.

## **Charm**
Determines the ability to charm and convince with one's words.

## **Perception**
Determines the capacity to observe details in the environment and listen carefully.

## **Memory**
Determines the capacity to retain and recall long-term knowledge.

## **Intuition**
Determines the capacity to understand or process information to derive further insights.

When a player **fails** an attribute check for a mental attribute, they gain **1 experience point** in it.
During a long rest, a player can choose a mental attribute in which they possess at least one experience point to **meditate**.
