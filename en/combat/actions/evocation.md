# **Evocation (Action)**

To perform an [**evocation**](/en/runes/evocation) it is first necessary to create and then use an [**arcane core**](/en/runes/arcane-core), inserting it into a rune circuit or manifesting it directly. Generating an arcane core requires **1 action** and can be done only during your turn, while inserting it into a rune circuit or manifesting it always requires **1 action**, but the action can be performed on your turn or in response to another action **outside your turn**. Evokers can maintain only **1 arcane core** at a time, and an arcane core generated on your turn **dissipates** at the start of your next turn.
