# **Attack Action**
During combat, to try to hit an enemy, it is necessary to use an **attack action**.  
To do so, the attacking character must first **choose a target** for their attack and **declare if they intend to hit a specific point** of their opponent. This type of attack is called a **precision strike** and allows the attacker to inflict additional effects on their opponent depending on the part hit, at the cost of making it harder to land the attack.  
Once the target is chosen and the type of attack declared, the character **uses 1 action** to launch the attack with an equipped weapon, but the attacked creature has the possibility of using one of its actions as a **reaction** to defend or counterattack.  
To determine if the attack hits the target, it is necessary to perform an attack roll, which consists of **rolling a d20** and **adding** to the result the score in the **characteristic attribute** of the weapon used (generally **STR** for melee weapons and **PRC** for ranged weapons) and comparing it with the **armor class (AC)** of the attacked creature. In the case of precision strikes, the dexterity modifier is applied twice to the armor class and a specific modifier is added, depending on the body part attacked.  
If the result of an attack roll **meets or exceeds the AC** of the target, the attack hits and damage calculation proceeds. The damage inflicted is different for each type of weapon and is described in their respective sheets.  
If an attack hits a vulnerable point or is performed with great mastery, it is possible to inflict much higher damage than normal. This happens when the attack is a **critical hit**, and it occurs automatically if the result (before modifiers) of the d20 in the attack roll **+ PRC** is **greater than 20**. All damage that would be inflicted by a critical hit is doubled.

# **Actions in Response to an Attack**
Every creature, when chosen as the target of an attack and **before the attack roll is made**, can use one of its actions as a **reaction** to try to defend itself and/or avoid the attack. A creature reacting to an attack can perform **one** of the following actions:  
1. **Dodge**: The attack roll is **reduced** by the **DEX** score of the attacked creature. A creature with movement equal to 0 **cannot** use this action.  
2. **Block**: If the attacked creature has a **shield** equipped, it can block the attack to **reduce** the result of the attack roll by its **CON** score.  
3. **Resist**: The damage potentially suffered from the attack is **halved**, and the attacked creature obtains **advantage** on saving throws caused by the attack.  
4. **Parry**: If the attacked creature has a **melee weapon** equipped, it can attempt to parry the attack. To do so, it must also roll a d20 + its score in the **same attribute as the weapon used by the attacker**. If the result is **greater** than the enemy's attack roll: the attack is parried and **deals no damage**, and the attacking creature is forced to end its turn. If the result is **lower** than the enemy's attack roll: the attack automatically hits and is considered a **critical hit**.
