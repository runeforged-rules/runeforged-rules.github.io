# **Combat Actions**

During a combat turn, each character has **3 actions** that can be used **during their turn** or as **reactions** in response to others' actions. Each type of action performed in combat can require a variable number of actions, specified in its description. Some moves are faster and require **1 action**, such as [attacking](./attack) or attempting to dodge enemy blows, while other more complex ones might require **2 or more**, such as reloading a weapon or performing an [evocation](./evocation).
During their turn, **for each action used** by a character, they can [move](./movement) a distance in meters **equal to their movement**, as long as the chosen action does not prevent it.

## **Combat Turn Order**
At the beginning of combat, to decide the turn order, each character determines their **initiative** score for the combat by rolling a **d20 + DEX**. The character with the highest initiative will take their turn first, followed by the turn of the creature with the second highest score, and so on in descending order. When the character with the lowest initiative has completed their turn, the turn passes again to the character with the highest initiative. In case of a tie, the character with the higher **DEX** has precedence.

# Common Actions
During combat, each creature has the following actions available during their turn, each with a cost of **1 action**:  
1. **Dash**: A creature's movement during the dash action is **doubled**.
2. **Jump**: Allows a creature to jump a distance equal to 1 meter + their **STR** score divided by 5. This distance can be covered horizontally or vertically. If the creature does not have the necessary space for a running start before jumping, the jump distance is **halved**. If preceded by the **dash** action, the horizontal distance is **doubled**.
3. **Gear Change**: The creature has access to their inventory to **equip or stow** a weapon of their choice.
4. **Stand Up**: If prone, the creature stands up from the ground.
