# **Movement and Position**
Whether it's to investigate a crime scene, explore an abandoned cave, or fight imposing monsters, every character needs to move, and the Runeforged system allows them to do so freely, without the use of a grid. 
Each move can be performed in a single straight line or multiple, but in combat, the distance traveled during an action must always be **less than or equal to the movement** of the character.
Some places have areas that are particularly complex to cross without adequate skills or equipment; this type of terrain is called **difficult terrain**, and every move performed by a character within it consumes **double the movement**.
In addition to the surrounding environment, there are other sources that can make moving complex, such as the effects of [evocations](./evocation) or the presence of other creatures. In fact, attempting to cross a space already occupied by another creature is equivalent to moving through **difficult terrain**.
